## Extracted Signals

- The component taxonomy follows Element Plus-like states: default, primary,
  success, warning, error, info, hover, press, disabled, loading, plain, text,
  link, round, circle, and size variants.
- The product shell uses a white left sidebar, pale blue top tab bar, dense
  English navigation labels, and a cool light gray workspace.
- Dashboard pages use KPI strips, donut charts, hot lists, and full-width order
  tables.
- Table pages combine a top filter form, compact data table, status chips,
  operation links, and pagination.
- Status language includes Success, Normal, Awaiting approval, Abnormal, In
  detail, Audits, Search, Reset, and Export.
